# Procurement policy
Version 2026-02. Owner: Finance (Irene Finance).

## Purpose
This policy sets who may approve purchases of goods and services and how suppliers are selected.

## Approval thresholds
| Total value of purchase | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 1,000 | Approval of the department head. No purchase order needed. |
| EUR 1,000 to EUR 10,000 | Approval of the Finance Director, two written quotes and a purchase order. |
| Above EUR 10,000 | Approval of the management committee, three written quotes, a purchase order and a contract reviewed by Legal. |

Purchases must not be split into smaller orders to avoid thresholds.

## Software and subscriptions
Software licences above EUR 200 per year need Finance approval in addition to the manager. Software requests start in the IT portal.

## Supplier checks
- Every supplier that handles Contoso or customer data goes through a security and privacy review, which takes up to 5 business days.
- Suppliers are paid on net 30 terms unless the contract says otherwise.
- Invoices are paid only against a purchase order or a signed contract.

## Signing contracts
Contracts with a yearly value up to EUR 50,000 may be signed by the Finance Director. Higher values are signed by the CEO. Employees may not sign contracts on behalf of Contoso.

## Conflicts of interest
Anyone with a personal interest in a supplier must tell the Finance Director and take no part in the decision.
