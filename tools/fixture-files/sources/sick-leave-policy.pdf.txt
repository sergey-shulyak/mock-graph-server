# Sick leave policy
Version 1.3, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: HR (Carol HR).

## Entitlement
Every employee receives up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year at full salary. Sick days do not reduce the 24 days of paid vacation. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## What to do when you are ill
- Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of illness, by phone or Slack message. Do not wait until the end of the day.
- If you are ill for 3 consecutive working days or fewer, no medical certificate is needed.
- From the 4th consecutive day, you must provide the number of your electronic sick leave certificate to HR within 2 working days of returning.
- Tell HR if the absence is expected to exceed 10 working days.

## After the paid days are used
After the 10 paid days, sick leave is paid under Ukrainian law. HR will explain the procedure and the amounts to the employee.

## Illness during vacation
If you fall ill during paid vacation and provide a certificate, the days of illness are not counted as vacation. The vacation days are returned and can be rescheduled with your manager.

## Caring for a sick child
Employees may take up to 3 working days per year, with a manager's approval, to care for a sick child. These days are paid from the vacation balance unless a certificate is provided.

## Privacy
Medical information is shared only with HR. Managers are told the dates of absence, not the diagnosis.
