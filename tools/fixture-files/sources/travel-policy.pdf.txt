# Business travel policy
Version 3.0, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance (Irene Finance).

## Booking
- Book all travel through the Contoso Travel portal at least 14 days before departure to get the best fares.
- Trips with a total cost above EUR 1,500 need pre-approval from the Finance Director.
- Your manager must approve the purpose of the trip before booking.

## Transport
- Flights shorter than 6 hours: economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer: premium economy.
- Trains: second class. Seat reservations are reimbursed.
- Private car: EUR 0.25 per km, with a mileage log.
- Taxis only when public transport is not practical or after 22:00.

## Accommodation and meals
- Hotels up to EUR 140 per night, including breakfast where offered.
- Meals while travelling: EUR 35 per day. No receipts are needed for meals within this per diem.

## During the trip
Keep your security basics: use the VPN on public Wi-Fi, never leave laptops in cars and report loss of a device within 1 hour.

## After the trip
Submit the expense claim within 30 days. Unused tickets must be reported to Finance within 5 days so that refunds can be requested.

## Cancellations
If a trip is cancelled, cancel bookings within 24 hours. Non-refundable costs caused by late cancellation may be charged to the department budget.
