# Project Phoenix charter
Sponsor and owner: Frank Manager. Version 1.2, approved 21 April 2026.

## Purpose
Rebuild the Contoso Portal, the customer-facing web application of Contoso Ledger, on a modern frontend stack that is faster, accessible and easier to change.

## Goals
- Reduce the Largest Contentful Paint to under 2.5 seconds at the 75th percentile.
- Increase the login-to-first-action conversion by 10 percent.
- Reduce support tickets about portal usability by 20 percent.
- Meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.

## Scope
In scope: login, dashboard, invoices, payments, bank feeds and settings pages. Out of scope for the first release: dark mode, mobile apps, and the reporting module of Contoso Pulse.

## Budget
The approved budget is EUR 420,000. It covers 9 team members and a UX contractor. Spending is reviewed monthly with the Finance Director.

## Team
Frank Manager (owner), Bob Frontend (frontend lead), Maya Newhire (frontend engineer, from October 2026), Grace QA (quality), Olga Contractor (UX designer, contractor), plus four backend and platform engineers part-time.

## Risks
Key risks are staffing, a dependency on API v2 stability and the interaction with the retirement of API v1 on 31 December 2026.
