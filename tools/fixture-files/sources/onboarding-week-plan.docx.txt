# First week plan for new hires
Owner: HR. Applies to all new employees; engineering teams add technical sessions.

## Monday
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Welcome at Reception, floor 5, with the HR coordinator |
| 10:30 | Badge, laptop handover and Okta Verify setup with IT |
| 12:30 | Lunch with your buddy |
| 14:00 | Team introduction with your manager |
| 16:00 | Read the employee handbook |

## Tuesday
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Security awareness course, part 1 (45 minutes) |
| 11:30 | Set up VPN, 1Password and Slack |
| 14:00 | Access requests for team systems |

## Wednesday
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Security awareness course, part 2 (45 minutes) |
| 11:30 | Product overview: Contoso Ledger and Contoso Pulse |
| 15:00 | HR session: policies and how to use the HR portal |

## Thursday
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 11:00 | First one-to-one with your manager |
| 14:00 | Team ceremony or standup |

## Friday
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 11:00 | Agree your 30-60-90 day plan |
| 16:00 | Week one feedback with HR |

All new hires must finish the security awareness course by Friday of week one.
