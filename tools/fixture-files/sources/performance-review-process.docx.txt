# Performance review process
Version 2026. Owner: HR (Carol HR).

## Overview
Contoso runs one annual review cycle with a mid-year check-in. The annual review decides ratings and salary adjustments for the next year.

## Calendar
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Goal setting | January, within the first 3 weeks |
| Mid-year check-in | 30 June |
| Self-review due | 5 December |
| Peer feedback due | 8 December |
| Manager review due | 15 December |
| Calibration meetings | 16 to 18 December |
| Results shared with employees | 22 December |
| Salary changes effective | 1 February |

## Ratings
Ratings use a five point scale.
- 1: Needs improvement
- 2: Partially meets expectations
- 3: Meets expectations
- 4: Exceeds expectations
- 5: Outstanding

Most employees are expected to land on 3 or 4. A rating of 5 requires a written justification approved by the head of department.

## Peer feedback
Each employee nominates at least 3 peers to give written feedback. The manager may add up to 2 more. Feedback is shared with the employee without names.

## Calibration
HR and the heads of department compare ratings across teams to keep them consistent. Managers do not tell employees their rating before calibration is finished.

## Performance improvement plan
An employee rated 1, or rated 2 twice in a row, receives a performance improvement plan lasting 60 days with fortnightly check-ins. The plan is written by the manager with HR.

## Appeals
An employee may ask HR to review a rating within 10 working days after results are shared.
